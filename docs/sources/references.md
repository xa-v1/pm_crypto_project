# References

One peer-reviewed source per diagnostic method. Citations are real and verifiable. Where the journal version is paywalled, the freely accessible pre-publication version is given. All DOIs should still be verified through Crossref or the publisher page before printing.

## Slide 10: Lead-Lag Cross-Correlogram (cluster bootstrap inference)

**Cameron, A. C., Gelbach, J. B., and Miller, D. L.** (2008). Bootstrap-based improvements for inference with clustered errors. *Review of Economics and Statistics*, 90(3), 414–427.
Standard reference for the cluster bootstrap used over contracts in the lead-lag CIs.
DOI: `10.1162/rest.90.3.414` (paywalled).

**Open access version.** Cameron, A. C., Gelbach, J. B., and Miller, D. L. (2007). "Bootstrap-Based Improvements for Inference with Clustered Errors." *NBER Technical Working Paper No. t0344.*
Freely accessible at `https://www.nber.org/papers/t0344`. Identical empirical content to the RES version.

## Slide 11: Variance Ratio Test

**Lo, A. W., and MacKinlay, A. C.** (1988). Stock market prices do not follow random walks: Evidence from a simple specification test. *Review of Financial Studies*, 1(1), 41–66.
The canonical variance-ratio test, including the Hurst-exponent reading off the slope of log VR(q) versus log q.
DOI: `10.1093/rfs/1.1.41` (paywalled).

**Open access version.** Lo, A. W., and MacKinlay, A. C. (1987). "Stock Market Prices Do Not Follow Random Walks: Evidence from a Simple Specification Test." *NBER Working Paper No. 2168.*
Freely accessible at `https://www.nber.org/papers/w2168`. Pre-publication version of the RFS paper.

## Slide 12: AUC Comparison via DeLong Test

**DeLong, E. R., DeLong, D. M., and Clarke-Pearson, D. L.** (1988). Comparing the areas under two or more correlated receiver operating characteristic curves: A nonparametric approach. *Biometrics*, 44(3), 837–845.
The canonical paired non-parametric test for comparing AUCs on the same out-of-sample observations.
DOI: `10.2307/2531595` (paywalled; JSTOR access via institutional library is standard).

No open pre-publication version exists for this paper. The JSTOR-hosted version on `https://www.jstor.org/stable/2531595` is the canonical link.
